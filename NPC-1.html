Field notes
  • Negative numbers: tap ± next to a field to flip it negative. Phone number pads often have no minus key.
  • Build both ends yourself. Light the Overworld portal at the destination and the Nether portal at the shown spot before traveling, so Bedrock never has to place one for you.
  • Directions: North is −Z, South is +Z, East is +X, West is −X.
  • Spacing: keep Nether portals more than 16 blocks apart and Overworld portals more than 128 blocks apart.
  • Height: keep each pair at a similar Y. The suggested Nether Y is kept between 5 and 120.
  • Coordinates on screen: world settings → Game → Show Coordinates.
